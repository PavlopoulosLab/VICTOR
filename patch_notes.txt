Patch notes

2026/11/21
-New static web application in docs/ (HTML and JavaScript, no server or R needed). All calculations run in the browser.
-12 additional comparison metrics (22 in total): Rand, F-Measure, Meila-Heckerman, normalized Van Dongen, B-Cubed precision/recall/F, NMI by larger entropy, Adjusted Mutual Information, normalized Variation of Information, Homogeneity, Completeness.
-5 further metrics (27 in total): Optimal-Match Accuracy (Hungarian), Element-Centric Similarity, Variation of Information by joint entropy, Omega Index and Overlapping NMI. The last two also compare overlapping clusterings.
-Export dialog for every plot: PNG, SVG, PDF, JPEG or WebP, up to 6x resolution, theme/white/transparent background.
-UpSet plot in the Files tab showing which objects the selected cluster sets share, when they differ.
-Metrics that are too costly for the loaded sets are skipped with a notice; large comparisons ask before running.
-Network quality is linked to the comparison: one table scores every loaded cluster set on the network, the Compare step shows it for the compared sets, and the home page presents both functions side by side.
-Home page that explains what VICTOR does and how to start; an "i" button next to every metric shows a one-line explanation on hover, focus or tap.
-Interface redesign: numbered steps (Load & prepare, Compare, Plots), one-click fixes when sets contain different objects, metric presets with one-line explanations, a summary of the most and least similar pair, results table with metrics or pairs as rows.
-Eight bundled examples with descriptions, including the myocardial infarction case study and four synthetic sets.
-Light theme by default with a switch to dark; the banner is an SVG that follows the theme.
-Cluster quality on a network: internal density, cut ratio, modularity and coverage next to conductance.
